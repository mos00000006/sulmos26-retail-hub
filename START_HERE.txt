5. Verify the LIVE URL:
   https://sulmos26-retail-hub.moyanamoses006.workers.dev/api/version

   https://sulmos26-retail-hub.moyanamoses006.workers.dev/api/setup-check

   OWNER_EXISTS: sign in instead of creating a second owner

   DB_MIGRATION_REQUIRED: D1 database schema needs migrations
